Simple Admission Process

We've made applying to MSV straightforward. Follow these steps to secure your child's place.

1
Submit Application

2
Schedule Visit

Admission Requirements

Application Form

  • Recent passport photo
Birth Certificate

Medical Records
  • Immunization records

  • Last term's report card
  • Transfer certificate (if applicable)
