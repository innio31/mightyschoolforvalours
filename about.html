Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

The guiding principles that shape everything we do at Mighty School for Valours.

Our Mission

To deliver excellent and all-round education to happy, sound and assertive intellectuals — equipping them with knowledge, skills and character to thrive.

Our Vision

To raise good citizens who will be literates, disciplined, hardworking and God-fearing through a broad and balanced education that promotes excellence and total well-being.

Our Values

Passion for Excellence · Strength from Hardwork · Courage and Honesty · Respect for People · Praying Always · Responsibility to Society.

Our Story

A Decade of Impact

Mighty School for Valours was founded with a clear conviction: that every Nigerian child deserves transformative education. Since 2013, that conviction has shaped our curriculum and community.
